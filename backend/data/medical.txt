Common symptoms and advice:
- Headache: Could be tension or dehydration. Rest and hydrate. See a doctor if severe.
- Fever: Often indicates infection. Rest and monitor temperature. Consult a doctor if above 103F.
- Cough: May be due to cold or allergies. Stay hydrated. Consult a doctor if persistent for weeks.
- Stomach ache: Can be indigestion or gas. Avoid heavy food. Seek help if pain is sharp/localized.
- Fatigue: Possible causes include lack of sleep or stress. Ensure 7-9 hours of sleep.
- Sore throat: Typically viral. Gargle with warm salt water.
- Rash: Could be allergic reaction or infection. Do not scratch. See a doctor if spreading.
- Dizziness: May be related to blood pressure or inner ear. Sit down immediately.
